2. Accounts and eligibility

You must be 18 or older to open and operate an account. A parent or guardian may order for a dependent and must be authorised to share that person’s information. Provide accurate contact and delivery details and keep your password, OTP and optional device PIN confidential.

A referral code is optional for customer registration. Do not create fake accounts, use another person’s identity, manipulate orders or share credentials. Contact support promptly if your account is used without permission.

3. Medicines and prescriptions

Product descriptions are information, not a diagnosis or prescription. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about treatment, dosage, interactions and suitability.

Where a prescription is required, supply a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner. Supply is subject to prescription review, applicable licensing requirements and availability. Uploading a prescription does not guarantee acceptance or dispatch. We may ask for clarification or decline an order that cannot lawfully or safely be fulfilled. Do not order restricted products through a channel that does not support their lawful supply.

4. Prices, offers and payment

Customer accounts are shown the applicable retail selling price. Verified business accounts may have separate authorised trade pricing. Product packing, quantity and unit shown for the selected item determine what is ordered; MRP and selling price are different amounts.

Prices are in INR. The checkout quote shows item prices, applicable tax treatment, delivery, platform or COD charges, coupon discounts, wallet use and the payable total. Review the final quote before placing the order. A coupon applies only when its server validation succeeds and its discount appears in that quote.

Payment options depend on the order and current availability. A payment confirmation is subject to verification by the payment provider. COD remains unpaid until receipt of cash is recorded. Do not share card PINs, OTPs or banking passwords with support.

5. Orders, cancellations and remedies

An order acknowledgement confirms receipt of the request. Fulfilment depends on stock, payment, prescription checks and serviceability. If an accepted item cannot be supplied, support will explain the cancellation or alternative; any substitution requires your agreement where applicable.

Request cancellation through support before dispatch. Cancellation after dispatch may not be possible. Returns, replacements, refunds and delivery issues follow the linked policies; statutory remedies remain available.
