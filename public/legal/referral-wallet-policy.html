Referral, Wallet & Withdrawal Policy

Three referral levels, genuine-sale credits and transparent withdrawal records.

1. Referral participation

A customer can use an optional referral code at registration. The saved sponsor relationship determines the referral chain used by the service. Do not promise a code can always be changed after registration; contact support about an incorrect relationship.

Referral income arises from eligible genuine product transactions, not from registration alone. No fixed income, salary, investment yield or guaranteed earning is promised. Do not use fake accounts, circular orders, misleading income claims, spam or unverified medical claims.

2. Current three-level calculation

The fixed referral rates are Level 1: 10%, Level 2: 3%, Level 3: 2%. These are referral levels, not an eight-tier income promise. Business distribution roles and trade margins are separate.

The current service calculates each level on the order’s recorded item line total (or the recorded item subtotal for legacy orders without item rows), rounded to two decimal places. It is not calculated on the full total including separate delivery/platform/COD fees. Only eligible active uplines receive an allocation; you are not entitled to every level’s percentage yourself.

Payment must be verified as paid and the order must not be cancelled. COD commission cannot be credited just because delivery is marked complete when cash is still recorded pending. A qualifying payment can be processed before delivery; cancellation/refund corrections remain possible. The base uses recorded product line totals; separately applied order-level coupon discounts do not currently reduce those line totals.

Commission history should identify the order, level, rate and credited amount. For example, a ₹1,000 recorded commission base gives ₹100 / ₹30 / ₹20 across the three eligible levels, before later transaction-specific corrections.

3. Wallet use and ledger

A wallet balance is a platform ledger for credited earnings, adjustments, payments and withdrawals. It is not a bank deposit and does not earn interest. Review available balance and credit/debit history; contact support with an order or transaction reference if an entry is missing.

Available wallet funds can be used for an eligible product purchase up to the accepted checkout amount. No withdrawal-related TDS deduction is applied by the current system merely for buying medicines with wallet funds. This describes platform treatment, not a personal tax exemption.

Cancellation/refund reversals and repayments must have corresponding records. A balance or referral relationship does not confer access to another buyer’s private invoice or prescription.

4. Bank withdrawal requests

Submit a request with correct account-holder name, bank account and IFSC using Wallet & Payouts. A submitted gross amount is reserved once and the available balance reduces accordingly. Do not submit duplicate requests because approval is taking time.

Requests progress through pending, approved, processing and success, or rejected/failed with a recorded reason. Approval alone is not a bank transfer. Success should have an actual bank/UTR reference. Rejected/failed reservations are released once; a completed transfer is not automatically reversible.

The current platform withdrawal calculation shows a 5% deduction labelled TDS and the net payout. For ₹1,000 gross, it shows ₹50 withheld and ₹950 net. This is the current platform setting, not a statement that 5% is the statutory rate for every person or transaction. Applicable tax law, category, thresholds and PAN/residency status govern the correct withholding/reporting. Contact support for the deduction basis, tax documentation or correction if required.

Do not share your bank-login password, card PIN or OTP. Bank-processing times vary; review the request status and contact support for a delayed or disputed payout.

5. Corrections and complaints

Keep the order number, commission row or withdrawal request ID when reporting a problem. Support must investigate identifiable records rather than make an unsupported blanket balance adjustment.

We do not guarantee commission on cancelled, fraudulent or otherwise ineligible transactions. Any recovery must be explained and recorded, subject to applicable law and your right to complain.
