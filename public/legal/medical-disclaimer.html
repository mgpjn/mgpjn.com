Medical & Prescription Information

Safe use of product information and prescription uploads.

1. Information is not treatment advice

MediGlaxo provides a medicine/healthcare catalogue and ordering services. Product names, composition, packing, strengths and descriptions are information to help identify a product; they are not a diagnosis, personalised treatment plan or proof that a product is suitable for you.

Consult a registered medical practitioner or qualified pharmacist about dosage, interactions, allergies, pregnancy, children and existing conditions. Do not start, stop or replace prescribed treatment solely because of a listing, discount or referral message.

2. Prescriptions and selection

Where a prescription is required, supply a clear, valid prescription and accurate patient details. Photograph/select only the intended document. Confirm that you have authority to share another patient’s information.

A prescription upload is a request for review and contact; it does not itself place a confirmed medicine order, guarantee stock, or establish a medical consultation. Any proposed change in medicine, strength or quantity needs appropriate confirmation.

3. Packaging, expiry and concerns

Check the actual product, seal, batch, expiry, strength and storage instructions on receipt. Do not use a damaged, expired or suspicious item. Contact support with the order and evidence; retain the packaging for investigation.

Do not rely on unverified cure, prevention or income-linked health claims made by a member. Report misleading claims or a medicine-quality concern to support. For urgent or severe symptoms, seek immediate professional/emergency medical care rather than waiting for a support reply.
