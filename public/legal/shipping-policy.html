Shipping & Delivery

Delivery charges, serviceability, dispatch checks and help with delayed orders.

1. Coverage and serviceability

Orders are supplied to supported delivery addresses in India. Product availability, courier coverage and applicable medicine-handling requirements determine whether an order can be accepted. An app or website listing is not a guarantee of delivery to every PIN code.

Temperature-sensitive or restricted products require suitable permitted handling. We do not promise universal express delivery or a verified cold-chain service for every item. Confirm suitability with support before ordering a product that needs special storage.

2. Charges shown before payment

The current standard delivery calculation is ₹50 for an item subtotal below ₹500 and ₹0 at ₹500 or above; supported takeaway/pickup orders have no delivery charge. Coupon eligibility may affect the displayed delivery charge.

Applicable platform/COD charges, if any, are shown separately in the final checkout quote. Verify the quoted charge for your order. Bulk/business shipments may need separately agreed arrangements; no unquoted freight charge should be treated as already accepted.

3. Dispatch and estimates

Dispatch depends on stock, payment verification, any required prescription review and a valid address. Support/order tracking will provide available dispatch and delivery information.

Delivery dates are estimates unless a specific commitment is expressly confirmed. Courier disruption, holidays, incorrect addresses, weather and required verification can cause delay. We do not make a blanket 24–48-hour delivery promise. If an agreed delivery schedule is missed, contact support for the remedies available under applicable law.

4. Receiving an order

Provide an accurate address and reachable phone number, and inspect visible packaging damage on arrival where possible. Never share an account-login or payment OTP as a delivery confirmation.

Report damage within seven calendar days with order details and clear photos/video under the Returns Policy. Contact support promptly about a missing, wrong or undelivered parcel. Do not consume suspicious products while a safety issue is being checked.

5. Tracking, failed delivery and support

Use order tracking/history or contact support@mgpjn.com / +91 9650582703 with your order number. Tracking events may update after the courier scans a parcel.

If delivery fails, support will explain available reattempt, return-to-origin, cancellation or refund options and any lawful, previously disclosed charge. A tracking status alone does not remove your right to raise a non-delivery complaint.
