Emergency Fund Terms

Eligibility, approval and repayment terms for the current repayable support feature.

1. Nature and amount

The MediGlaxo Family Emergency Fund feature currently offers a request for ₹5,000 repayable support at 0% interest, with no fund fee or late penalty in the current service rules. It is not a gift, insurance benefit, guaranteed entitlement, deposit or investment return.

The label “Emergency Fund” does not determine its legal or regulatory classification. Any applicable financial-service, consumer or app-store requirement continues to apply. Do not treat this page as evidence of an RBI licence or regulatory exemption.

2. Eligibility and approval

The current count requirement is at least 99 unique active eligible members across your first three referral levels, including at least 27 direct members at Level 1. The 27 direct members are part of the total 99, not an additional 27. Levels 2 and 3 may supply the remaining count.

Administrative/owner/employee and inactive accounts are excluded under the service rules. The eligibility page shows achieved and pending counts. Counts do not guarantee approval: account eligibility, an existing request, repayment cooldown, fund availability and administrative review also apply.

One active requested/approved/processing/disbursed fund is allowed at a time. A new request becomes possible only after full repayment plus a 30-day cooldown and a fresh eligibility check.

3. Request, disbursement and due date

Provide a truthful reason and accurate bank details and review the terms shown before confirming a request. The accepted terms version and request record are retained. Do not upload unrelated medical documents or credentials.

A request or approval does not mean money has reached your bank. Disbursement must be confirmed with the recorded payment reference. Full repayment is due one calendar month after confirmed disbursement under the current accepted service terms; see your recorded due date.

For a ₹5,000 disbursement under these rules, the total scheduled repayment is ₹5,000 with ₹0 interest and ₹0 fund/late fee. No new referral or product purchase should be represented as a substitute for a recorded repayment.

4. Repayment and records

Use the repayment methods shown for your fund. Wallet repayment requires your explicit confirmation and a matching debit/repayment record. Do not assume the service can silently take money from your wallet or bank.

Manual bank repayment is available only when official receiving instructions are configured and displayed. Use those instructions, retain a real bank reference, and submit it for verification; a payment screenshot alone does not prove money was received. Payment-gateway repayment is credited after provider verification.

Part payments reduce the recorded outstanding amount; the request closes only when full repayment is confirmed. Contact support before the due date if you need help or dispute a record. No harassment or access to your private contacts is authorised by these terms.

5. Questions and protection

Contact support@mgpjn.com or +91 9650582703 with the fund request ID. Do not pay an agent an advance approval fee or share an OTP. A count/progress bar, referral message or approval screen does not override the accepted terms or applicable law.
