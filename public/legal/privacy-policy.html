1. Scope and privacy contact

This policy covers mgpjn.com and Mediglaxo Pharma (Mgpjn), com.mgpjn.app, operated under the name MEDIGLAXO PHARMA. Contact support@mgpjn.com or +91 9650582703 for privacy requests. Business location: Sachin GIDC, Surat, Gujarat 394230, India; GSTIN 24ABVFM0075D1ZA.

Data handled depends on the features you use. You may browse public information without creating an account. Optional health, bank and business documents should be provided only for the service that needs them.

2. Information you provide

Account and contact details: name, mobile number, email, login credentials, delivery addresses and profile information. We also retain authentication/session records needed to operate and protect the account.

Prescription services: selected photos/files, patient name, mobile number and any doctor or medicine notes you submit. This can include health information. Obtain the patient’s permission before submitting another person’s prescription.

Orders and payments: items, quantity, price, addresses, invoice and order history, payment method, provider references, payment/fulfilment status and support correspondence. Card, UPI and banking authentication is handled through the relevant payment provider; never send a card PIN, CVV, banking password or OTP to support.

Member and business services: sponsor/referral relationships, relevant team information, commission and wallet credit/debit records, payout requests, bank holder/account/IFSC details, business/GST/drug-licence documents where requested, and Emergency Fund applications, reasons, disbursements and repayment references.

3. Device information and local storage

The website uses browser local/session storage for login sessions, cart, referral attribution and optional quick-login settings. Clearing this data may sign you out or remove a saved cart/referral code.

The app stores account/session and feature preferences on the device, including optional quick-login, referral attribution and update/rating-prompt state. Android Play Install Referrer can supply a referral code associated with an install.

Where you enable biometric login, the operating system handles biometric matching. MediGlaxo does not receive your fingerprint image or face template. Camera/gallery access is used when you choose to capture or select an upload; permission can be denied or withdrawn in device settings.

Our service and providers may process technical data such as IP address, device/browser information, timestamps and error/security records to operate, diagnose and protect the platform.

4. Why information is used

To register and authenticate users; validate prescriptions where required; respond to enquiries; calculate prices and offers; fulfil orders; arrange delivery; provide invoices and transaction updates; and handle cancellations, refunds and complaints.

To attribute referrals, show relevant authorised team information, calculate genuine-sale commissions, maintain wallet ledgers, process withdrawals, and assess/manage requested Emergency Fund support and repayments.

To prevent fraud, investigate disputes, maintain accounting/tax/medicine-sale records and comply with applicable legal obligations. Consent for optional services can be withdrawn by contacting support; processing already required for a transaction or legal record may continue for that purpose.

5. Who receives information

Authorised operations, pharmacy, customer-support, finance and administrative personnel receive information relevant to their work. Authorised referral/business-hierarchy views can include member names, contact details, sponsor relationships, team activity and wallet/earnings totals as provided by the service. Do not assume all profile/contact information is hidden from your authorised hierarchy.

Payment providers, including Razorpay when used, process payment and related verification information under their own privacy terms. Firebase Authentication/reCAPTCHA is used where the website’s phone sign-in requires it. Hosting, delivery and communication providers receive the information needed for the service they perform.

Information may be disclosed to competent authorities when required by law, or to investigate fraud and protect lawful rights. We do not offer prescription or health records for sale to advertisers. External sites, email providers and app stores apply their own policies when you open them.

6. Security, retention and deletion

Use HTTPS service connections, protect your device and do not disclose login credentials. Access is managed according to operational roles; no internet service can promise absolute security. Report suspected unauthorised disclosure to support.

We keep personal data while needed for the requested service, account administration and legitimate legal, accounting, security or dispute purposes. Upon a verified deletion request, account access and data no longer needed are removed or de-identified; merely disabling login is not the whole deletion process.

Necessary invoice, tax, payment, medicine-sale, dispute and fraud-prevention records may be retained for the period required for their specific legal purpose, with access limited to that purpose. A pending order, refund, wallet balance or fund repayment must be reconciled; that does not justify indefinite retention of unrelated profile or health data.

Use the Account & Data Deletion page to initiate a request inside or outside the app. You can also request access, correction, consent withdrawal or information about retained records through support. We may verify control of the registered contact without asking for your password or OTP.

7. Children and changes

The service is intended for account holders aged 18 or older. A parent/guardian submitting a child’s information must have authority to do so and should provide only the information needed for the requested service. Contact support to remove information submitted without authority.

The policy date/version identifies this notice. Material privacy changes will be reflected in this policy and relevant service notices.
