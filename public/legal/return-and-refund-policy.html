Returns, Refunds & Cancellation

Report delivery damage to support within seven days, with order details and clear evidence.

1. Damage complaints: seven-day window

For a product received damaged, contact support within 7 calendar days of delivery. Email support@mgpjn.com with the order number, registered contact, product/quantity affected, delivery date and clear photos and/or a video showing the damage, product/batch/expiry label and packaging. You may call +91 9650582703 for help.

Keep the item, invoice and packaging safely while support reviews the claim. Do not consume a damaged or suspect medicine. Do not return or courier medicine without support instructions. An unboxing video is helpful when available, but absence of a video does not by itself remove statutory rights.

2. What this process covers

The standard discretionary return/replacement process is for products received damaged, after support verification. There is no general change-of-mind, unused-stock or unsold-business-inventory return promise under this policy.

Opened, used, temperature-sensitive or hygiene-sensitive goods cannot ordinarily be accepted back for resale. This safety restriction does not eliminate a valid complaint about damage, defect, an expired/spurious item, a wrong/misdescribed product, non-delivery or another remedy required by law. Report these concerns promptly to the same support contact.

The seven-day damage-reporting process does not override a longer or otherwise mandatory right available under applicable consumer law. Support may request reasonable further evidence and will explain the proposed resolution.

3. Review and resolution

Support will check the order and submitted evidence and provide instructions for inspection, collection, replacement or refund, as appropriate. A return request is not automatically approved when an email is drafted or an attachment is selected.

For a verified fulfilment error or damaged delivery attributable to us, necessary return/replacement handling will be arranged without imposing an undisclosed charge. Do not send an item back until the return address and method are confirmed.

4. Cancellation

Contact support immediately to request cancellation before dispatch. A cancellation request after dispatch may not be possible; support will explain the available resolution.

If we cancel an item that cannot be fulfilled, the amount actually paid for that unfulfilled item is eligible for the applicable refund. Refund and cancellation records should be matched to the original order/payment.

5. Refund method and timing

For an approved refund, support will confirm the amount, method and expected timeline. We normally aim to initiate approved refunds within 3–5 business days after required verification/receipt; the payment provider or bank may take additional time to settle it. This is a processing target, not a promise of instant bank credit.

Prepaid amounts are ordinarily refunded to the original payment source. A COD refund needs a verified bank/UPI destination agreed with support. Wallet-funded portions are reconciled against the wallet ledger; an external payment is not forced into wallet credit without your agreement unless legally required.

Any related coupon/commission adjustment must match the cancelled/refunded transaction. A legitimate complaint cannot be rejected solely because a promotion was used. Ask support for the refund reference if the expected settlement date passes.
