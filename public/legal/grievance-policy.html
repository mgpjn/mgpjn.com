Use email for a written complaint and keep a copy. Include your name, registered contact, relevant order/withdrawal/fund ID, a concise description and requested resolution. Send only information necessary to investigate; do not email passwords, OTPs, card PINs or unrelated health records.

2. Damage evidence and attachments

For delivery damage, email within seven calendar days and attach clear photos and/or video of the affected item and packaging, with product/batch/expiry details. If your video is too large, contact support about a suitable secure method; do not publish private prescriptions or bank details in a public link.

The website contact action opens an email draft. It does not send the complaint or upload an attachment automatically. Add the evidence in your email app and send it to complete the request.

3. Response and escalation

Complaints are to be acknowledged within 48 hours and addressed within 30 days of receipt. Keep your complaint reference/correspondence and reply on the same thread if escalation is needed.

If unresolved, you may seek assistance from the National Consumer Helpline at consumerhelpline.gov.in or approach the appropriate consumer authority/commission under applicable law. These contact steps do not take away a legal remedy. Privacy requests and account deletion can also be initiated through the Account & Data Deletion page.
