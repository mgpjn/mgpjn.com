Account & Data Deletion

Request deletion of your MediGlaxo account and associated personal data.

Updated 20 November 2026 · MG-POLICIES-2026-11-20-v1

On this page
  1. How to request
  2. Verification and response
  3. What is removed and what may be retained

1. How to request

Email support@mgpjn.com with subject “Account Deletion Request”. Include your registered mobile number or email and your full name so the account can be located. You can initiate the same request from the app’s Profile/Policies area. You do not need to install the app to request deletion.

The email action on this page opens your email app; you must send the email yourself. If no email app is configured, use webmail with the address and subject above, or call +91 9650582703 for help. Never send your password, PIN, OTP or full payment-card details.

2. Verification and response

Support may ask you to confirm control of the registered contact using a proportionate verification process. Account access is not required to read this page or initiate a request.

We acknowledge consumer/privacy complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days, subject to any applicable legal period and necessary verification. Support will explain any specific delay or retained-record obligation rather than promise unverified immediate deletion.

3. What is removed and what may be retained

Following verification, personal profile/login data, saved addresses and other account-linked data that are no longer needed are deleted or de-identified. A deleted account may lose access to order history, referral participation and saved settings; ask for any records you need first. Deactivation alone is not the completion of deletion.

Necessary order/invoice, tax, medicine-sale, payment, wallet/payout, Emergency Fund, dispute and fraud-prevention records may be retained for their specific legal/accounting purpose. Support can explain the categories and applicable retention requirement. Retention is limited to that purpose and does not justify keeping unrelated data indefinitely.

Pending orders/refunds, legitimate wallet funds and any repayable support need reconciliation. Request assistance to settle these records; deleting an account does not cancel a lawful repayment obligation or remove your right to a valid refund.
